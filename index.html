a little about me

About me

I'm a Ph.D. researcher in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Maine and a research assistant in the SIEGE Research Lab, working with Dr. Prabuddha Chakraborty.

My work sits where AI security meets efficient machine learning. I probe risks in large language models and multi-agent systems, and I build neural architecture search methods that make AI practical on resource-constrained hardware.

I earned my B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Islamic University, Bangladesh, and my M.S. in Computer Engineering at UMaine.
